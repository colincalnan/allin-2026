Sept 16 to 17 · Palais des congrès, Montréal
7,500 attendees · 200+ speakers · 5 tracks · 11 industry verticals

Say this, not your job title

I build AI systems that generate media end to end. One wrote and published social content for financial advisors across Canada. Another turns a two hour gig recording into a month of captioned reels. Day job is AI transformation at CANARIE.

Why that line matters. Lead with the official title and every conversation for two days becomes a security conversation. You will go home having had the wrong forty chats. The line above is true, specific, has numbers in it, and routes people to the half of your work you want more of.

The three that matter

Do not miss

Creative Industries

Agora stage · David Lerech (Propulsion Media) + an exec from Runway

The most on-trajectory session in the building, and it nearly did not get found. Runway makes one of the video models in the render stack that the roles you want are built on. Sit near the front and catch them coming off stage. Two minutes with someone who has actually shipped a render pipeline beats an hour of panel.

Ask: what does the pipeline actually look like on the render side, and where does a model make the visual call rather than a human.

Highest value

Cohere

Exhibiting · Aidan Gomez (CEO, co-founder) and Joelle Pineau (Chief AI Officer) both speaking

Canadian, publishes its bands, and the agents and automations work is the closest thing on the floor to what you actually build. Individual contributor, no reports.

Work the booth, not the keynote. Engineers and recruiters, never the CEO. One good conversation with someone on the agents team is worth more than a photograph.

Your unfair advantage

Langfuse

Exhibiting · LLM observability and evals

The one booth where you arrive with something to say instead of being a punter. You built an eval harness last week that found a 6.7% groundedness failure and a contradiction between a rubric and its own schema. Two weeks ago evals was the weakest line in your material. Turning up at the eval company with real findings closes it.

Lead with the finding, not the tool.

Worth the walk

Covers the day job

Alliance Canada

Digital Research Alliance of Canada · exhibiting

CANARIE's sibling in national research infrastructure. One conversation here justifies the entire trip to Steve, and it feeds the AI transformation remit directly.

Creative industry, in a suit

Publicis · L'Oréal

Both exhibiting

The only two genuine creative-industry giants on the floor, both running real generative content operations at scale. Publicis has a Montréal office. This is the vertical-media lane wearing a lanyard.

Local ecosystem

Mirego · Osedea · Valtech · Walter Interactive

Québec design-and-build studios, all exhibiting

The creative-technical corner of Montréal. Worth twenty minutes if the floor is quiet. These are the shops that hire people who design and build.

Base covering

Microsoft · CrowdStrike · Delinea · Cyera · Qohash

Cybersecurity is a full vertical this year

Microsoft for the Copilot agent work specifically. The rest only if you want ammunition for the IT turf fight. Do not let this become the whole conference.

Evenings

Confirmed

AI Salon Montréal

Tues 16 Sept · AX.C · doors 17:30 · 331 going

17:45 welcome · 18:00 keynote · 18:15 startup demos and pitches · 19:00 networking. Hosts: Nicholas Nadeau, Claude G. Théoret, Sylvain Carle. Founders, builders and investors. Sponsored by Québec Tech and AX.C.

Go for the 19:00 hour, not the keynote. This is the room where Montréal's actual builders are, and it is the closest thing to the creative-technical ecosystem you will get all week.

Pending approval

MINDSHIFT 5@8

Tues 16 Sept, 17:00 to 20:00 · Bar Le Mal Nécessaire, 1015 Rue St Alexandre

Presented by Paperminds and Draft & Goal. Their framing question: how do we become better with technology, rather than simply better at using it?

The audience is marketing leaders, which is exactly why you should go. You will be one of very few people in that room who has actually built the thing everyone else is speculating about. That is your tech-and-marketing crossover in a bar.

Both are on the same evening and both start around five. AI Salon is confirmed, so if MINDSHIFT lands, do MINDSHIFT first from five and move to AI Salon for the networking hour at seven. They are a ten minute walk apart.

Ground rules

  • Hallways over seats. The talks are recorded, the conversations are not. Bias hard toward the floor and the Startup Zone.
  • The five tracks are AI & Society, Applied AI & Enterprise Transformation, AI at the Frontier, AI Data & Infrastructure, Physical AI & Robotics. Applied AI is your day job on a stage. Everything creative sits inside the industry verticals, not the tracks, which is why it is easy to miss.
  • Posting from a conference is the least job-seekery content that exists. A free week of being publicly an AI person with none of the risk.
  • Do not collect badges. Five real conversations beats fifty cards.
