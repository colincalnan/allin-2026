Two dates that matter more than the conference

Free, online, 30 Sept

SAT Research-Creation Residency, info session

Tue 30 Sept · 11:00 Montréal time · online residences@sat.qc.ca

The Société des arts technologiques runs a research-creation residency: $10,000, two months, access to their labs and teams, open to professional artists and collectives worldwide. One of its three research areas is interactive, real-time generative audiovisual technology using AI, built around Koaia, SAT's own real-time tool running Stable Diffusion with TensorRT.

A publicly funded institution has built a real-time diffusion tool for a 350 seat dome and pays outsiders to come and push it. The info session costs nothing and needs no preparation.

Why these sit above the conference. Across Moment Factory, PixMob, Rodeo FX, Behaviour, Cirque, the SAT, PHI and Zú there were roughly 170 live postings and not one remote senior engineering role, and no policy anywhere permitting a BC-based remote hire. Several require French. The employment route into this world is closed from Lake Country right now. The residency route is open, is paid, needs no portfolio in their domain because it is research rather than a job, and produces exactly the artifact that is missing.
The thirty second version

I do AI transformation at CANARIE, and I build generative media pipelines: systems that take a data source and produce finished images, video and copy on a schedule. One ran social content for financial advisors across Canada. Another turns a two hour live recording into a month of captioned reels.

Five people, and why

Two messages before you fly, three people to find on the floor. Not twelve. None of these five are speaking, so every one is a floor or networking conversation, which makes messaging beforehand more important rather than less.
Message first

Sarah Mackenzie

Director, MUTEK Forum and Creative Development · MUTEK

MUTEK is the Montréal non-profit founded in 2000 to promote live electronic music and real-time audiovisual performance. She runs the professional strand, so she knows every studio and every freelance real-time person in the city.

The question: MUTEK's AI Ecologies Lab brings together "artists, researchers, and technologists", six participants, a virtual lab then a week in Montréal including a sprint at the SAT. Can a technologist apply without an artist collaborator, and when does the next call open? Nobody could answer that from the public pages, which makes it the single best question to ask anyone next week.

Message first

Brigitte Monneau

Directrice générale · Bridge Média, holding the management mandate for E-AI

E-AI is the Québec community joining the entertainment and AI industries, formed by more than 50 organisations. Moment Factory and the SAT are confirmed current partners, and Céline Mornet of Moment Factory holds a board seat. She runs the front door to the entire ecosystem rather than one company in it.

The real target is E-AI 2027, 25 and 26 February at this same venue. Meeting her now, with no ask, is how you arrive there next year already known.

Find on the floor

Jean-Michaël Celerier

Director of Technology Development · Société des arts technologiques

The SAT builds its own software and publishes it: LivePose (silhouette and pose recognition from video streams, driving output), Satellite XR and Scenic. Their stack includes GStreamer and FFmpeg, which is your stack. They run the Satosphère, an 18 metre dome with 8 projectors and 98 audio channels that has produced more than 180 immersive works.

LivePose is camera in, pose out, driving visuals. That is the interactive performance primitive, and it is open. A question about their technical delivery spec is an ordinary thing for an engineer to ask.

Ground rules

  • Hallways over seats. The talks are recorded, the conversations are not. Bias hard toward the floor and the Startup Zone.
  • The five tracks are AI & Society, Applied AI & Enterprise Transformation, AI at the Frontier, AI Data & Infrastructure, Physical AI & Robotics. Applied AI is your day job on a stage. Everything creative sits inside the industry verticals, not the tracks, which is why it is easy to miss.
  • Post from the floor. Two days of good material and a reason to write something that is not a build log.
  • Do not collect badges. Five real conversations beats fifty cards.
  • Nothing here rests on next Wednesday. The conference is early in the sequence, not the moment. Learn now, build the artifact this autumn, and arrive at E-AI 2027 in February with something to show and a few faces already known.
  • No ask, ever. A conversation where you want nothing is the only kind that is easy to have, and it is a position that expires the moment you need something.
