The thirty second version

I do AI transformation at CANARIE, and I build generative media pipelines: systems that take a data source and produce finished images, video and copy on a schedule. One ran social content for financial advisors across Canada. Another turns a two hour live recording into a month of captioned reels.

The three that matter

Do not miss

Creative Industries

Agora stage · David Lerech (Propulsion Media) + an executive from Runway

Wed 16 Sept · time TBC in the app Palais des congrès de Montréal, 1001 Place Jean-Paul-Riopelle, Montréal QC H2Z 1H5

Part of the Media and Entertainment vertical, covering journalism, advertising, film, music, design, gaming and digital creation. Runway makes one of the video generation models that most of this field is built on, so this is the single most relevant hour of the two days.

Worth asking: what does the pipeline actually look like on the render side, and where in it does a model make the visual call rather than a person.

The time was not published in advance. Find it in the app first thing on Wednesday. There is a reminder in the calendar file for exactly that.

Something to say

Langfuse

Exhibiting · LLM observability and evals

Both days · exhibitor floor Palais des congrès de Montréal, 1001 Place Jean-Paul-Riopelle, Montréal QC H2Z 1H5

Evals are the part of LLM engineering with the least written about it and the most to argue about. I have just built a harness over a live LLM judge that found a 6.7 percent groundedness failure and a contradiction between a rubric and its own schema, so there is a real conversation to have here rather than a demo to watch.

Lead with the finding, not the tool.

Ground rules

  • Hallways over seats. The talks are recorded, the conversations are not. Bias hard toward the floor and the Startup Zone.
  • The five tracks are AI & Society, Applied AI & Enterprise Transformation, AI at the Frontier, AI Data & Infrastructure, Physical AI & Robotics. Applied AI is your day job on a stage. Everything creative sits inside the industry verticals, not the tracks, which is why it is easy to miss.
  • Post from the floor. Two days of good material and a reason to write something that is not a build log.
  • Do not collect badges. Five real conversations beats fifty cards.
