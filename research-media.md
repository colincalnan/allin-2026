# ALL IN 2026: Media and Entertainment Contacts

Research date: 2026-09-08. Conference: ALL IN 2026, Palais des congrès de Montréal, September 16-17 2026, co-organised by SCALE AI and Mila, 200+ speakers and 6,500+ attendees ([Tech Forum](https://techforum.ca/posts/all-in-2026-montral-ai-event), [NVIDIA event page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/events/all-in/)).

Lens: does this person or company build generative media pipelines, or could they hire someone who does, for a remote individual contributor role.

## Two corrections before anything else

1. **"Charles Cluzel, CMO, Radio-Canada" appears to be Christophe Cluzel, Senior Director of Marketing Communications.** No person named Charles Cluzel surfaced at Radio-Canada in any search. Christophe Cluzel exists, holds that title, and has publicly said he is speaking at ALL IN ([LinkedIn](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/christophe-cluzel-827a1297), [Association Marketing Québec profile](https://associationmarketingquebec.ca/profil/christophe-cluzel/)). Treat these as the same person until proven otherwise, and note the title is Senior Director, not CMO.

2. **Angle Media Group is Toronto, not Montreal.** Registered at 394 Euclid Ave, Toronto, with a studio address at 1227 Dundas St W ([LinkedIn company page](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/angle-media-group), [anglemediagroup.com](https://www.anglemediagroup.com/)).

## 1. Charles Trahan, Directeur Innovation & IA, Québecor Média

### What is verified

His LinkedIn headline reads "Directeur Innovation & IA · Québecor" ([ca.linkedin.com/in/charlestrahan1](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/charlestrahan1)). That is the entirety of what is publicly verifiable about this specific person. LinkedIn blocks automated fetching (HTTP 999), so I could not read the profile body, and no article, talk, interview, press release, or conference bio naming him in this role surfaced in any search.

**Do not confuse him with the other Charles Trahan.** There is a Montreal game and motion designer named Charles Trahan with a public portfolio at [charlestrahan.com](https://charlestrahan.com/) who worked at NumériQ, a Quebecor division, on AR/VR from 2019 to 2020 ([portfolio Quebecor page](https://charlestrahan.com/game-design/quebecor/)). That is a **different man**: his portfolio links to [linkedin.com/in/charles-trahan/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/charles-trahan/), not `charlestrahan1`, and his own About page says he has been at Ubisoft from 2021 to present ([about page](https://charlestrahan.com/about/)). Several search summaries conflate the two. Do not walk up to the Québecor Trahan and mention his VR distillery work for the SAQ.

### The company

Québecor is a large Quebec conglomerate: broadcasting, telecom, media, publishing, sports and entertainment. Roughly 8,832 employees as of 2022 and about $5.68B annual revenue in FY2025 ([Craft.co profile](https://craft.co/quebecor)). Media arm includes Groupe TVA, Le Journal de Montréal, QUB, and out-of-home advertising ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebecor)). Overwhelmingly Quebec and Canada focused, francophone, Montreal headquartered.

Group TVA has been cutting hard: nearly 800 layoffs since 2022 and public warnings about the future of Quebec television ([Le Devoir](https://www.ledevoir.com/culture/medias/979748/groupe-tva-sonne-nouveau-alarme-avenir-tele-quebecoise)). That matters. A media division in contraction hires differently than one in expansion.

### Build or buy

**Unverified either way.** No public Québecor announcement about generative AI tooling in content production surfaced. Historically NumériQ and COLAB STUDIO were genuine in-house production and innovation shops doing AR/VR ([COLAB STUDIO](https://www.colabstudio.ca/en/)), which shows the company has a habit of building creative technology internally, but I found nothing tying that to generative AI in 2025 or 2026.

### Verdict

The title is the most on-target of anyone on this list. The evidence base is the thinnest. Treat this as a high-value unknown to be resolved in person, not a researched lead.

## 3. Brigitte Monneau, Bridge Média / Entertain-AI

**This is the strongest lead on the list, and not for the reason it was flagged.**

### They are two things, and the relationship is the interesting part

**Bridge Média** is her own consultancy. It supports creative industries and non-profits with strategy, partnership development and interim management. She is its founder ([PRIX NUMIX jury bio](https://numix.ca/membresjury2026/brigitte-monneau/), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bmonneau/)).

**Entertain-AI (E-AI)** is a separate organisation: an international B2B community sitting at the intersection of entertainment and artificial intelligence, launched in Quebec, with its first gathering at the Palais des congrès in June 2022 ([Palais des congrès partnership announcement](https://congresmtl.com/en/the-palais-des-congres-de-montreal-partners-with-entertain-ai-an-international-b2b-community-that-brings-together-entertainment-and-artificial-intelligence/)). It was founded by roughly 50 Quebec businesses and organisations including Moment Factory, Behaviour Interactive, Moov AI, The 7 Fingers, IVADO, Centre Phi and SAT ([E-AI announcement](https://entertain-ai.com/e-ai-50-quebec-businesses-and-organizations-create-the-first-community-representing-the-entertainment-and-ai-industries-of-tomorrow/)).

**The link:** E-AI retained Bridge Média to provide fractional general management of the organisation, and Monneau leads that mandate, including preparation for E-AI 2027. This wording appears on her LinkedIn profile ([linkedin.com/in/bmonneau](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bmonneau/)); I could not fetch the page directly, so it is sourced from indexed profile text rather than a press release. Her NUMIX bio independently confirms she sits on E-AI's executive committee ([NUMIX](https://numix.ca/membresjury2026/brigitte-monneau/)).

So: she is not an employee of a media company. She is the outsourced managing director of Quebec's generative-AI-and-creativity industry body. Functionally she is a hub, not a hiring manager.

### Her background

Over 25 years in Canada's creative industries, with a background in information and communication technology law. Leadership roles at Téléfilm Canada, the Alliance of Women in Creative Industries (Allia), and SYNTHÈSE Pôle Image Québec. She was Directrice générale of FCTMN (Femmes du cinéma, de la télévision et des médias numériques) from October 2017 ([Grenier aux nouvelles](https://www.grenier.qc.ca/nouvelles/14088/brigitte-monneau-devient-directrice-generale-des-fctmn), [CTVM](https://ctvm.info/brigitte-monneau-nouvelle-directrice-generale-a-fctmn-des-octobre-2017/)) and departed in January 2021 ([Lien MULTIMÉDIA](https://lienmultimedia.com/spip.php?article79109=&debut_dossier_10DerniersArticles=96)). She also sits on the Academy of Cinema and Television Quebec section and the advisory committee to Concordia University's president ([NUMIX](https://numix.ca/membresjury2026/brigitte-monneau/)).

Her own expertise is strategy, governance, government relations and partnerships. **She is not technical**, and nothing suggests she personally works on generative AI.

### Why E-AI matters enormously to you

I read the current E-AI programme and speaker list directly. This is not a policy talkshop. It is exactly your world:

- The 2026 theme was "On se retrousse les manches" (roll up our sleeves), explicitly framed as a critical creation camp showing prototypes, bugs and failures rather than polished theory ([entertain-ai.com](https://entertain-ai.com/)).
- The programme includes hands-on generative image workshops with Midjourney, DALL-E and Stable Diffusion ([entertain-ai.com](https://entertain-ai.com/)).
- **Conner Tozier**, senior animation designer at Moment Factory, is a speaker described as having moved into **developing generative AI tools built specifically for artists**, including generative AI visual content for Phish at the Sphere in Las Vegas ([speaker list](https://www.entertain-ai.com/intervenants)). That is a generative media pipeline built in house, by a Montreal company, for a stadium show.
- **David Usher** (Reimagine AI), **David Bennett** (Mimic Productions, real-time conversational AI avatars, facial animation and motion capture patents), **Benoît Carré / SKYGGE** (AI pop music since 2015), **Cloë St Jours** (AI film director), **Alexandre Stevens** (built Bookr.fm SaaS and Predikt.live decision layer for festival programming), **Audric Gagnon** (experimenting.ai, teaches AI in audiovisual production at L'inis), **Coralie Ratieuville** (ARTE's AI project group) ([speaker list](https://www.entertain-ai.com/intervenants)).
- **ALCHIMIA**, an initiative of Prompt in collaboration with E-AI, is handing out **$100,000 in grants** to creators, startups and studios working with AI, with 12 semi-finalists pitching on the E-AI stage in front of 1,000+ people ([entertain-ai.com](https://entertain-ai.com/)).
- **E-AI 2027 is confirmed for February 25-26 2027 at the Montreal Convention Centre** ([Weezevent listing](https://my.weezevent.com/grand-evenement-e-ai-2026-annual-conference)).

Monneau is the person running the organisation that convenes all of the above, and she is currently building the 2027 programme.

### The Rocky Ozaki connection

**I could not verify any link between Rocky Ozaki and Brigitte Monneau.** Multiple targeted searches returned nothing connecting them, and Ozaki does not appear on E-AI's speaker or advisory listings I could reach. That is a null result, not a disproof: LinkedIn's mutual-connection graph is not publicly searchable, so if the connection came from LinkedIn it would be invisible to me.

Highest priority on this list. Not as a hiring manager, as the gatekeeper to the Quebec generative media community and to E-AI 2027.

## 4. MediaTonik

### What the company does

MediaTonik is a Montreal advertising network (régie publicitaire) representing Canadian publishers and their ad inventory. Their own site claims 93+ Canadian publishers across eight content categories, 45M+ monthly page views, 15M+ monthly users and 75+ audience segments. Brands represented include Chatelaine, TV5Unis, Jobboom and Les Affaires. Offices at 1020 Rue de Bleury, Montreal ([mediatonik.ca](https://mediatonik.ca/)). They acquired Obox's ad network in 2023 ([Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2023/04/11/mediatonik-acquires-oboxs-ad-network/)), took on Médialo's programmatic ad sales in 2025 ([Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2025/05/22/industry-news-mediatonik-medialo/)) and partnered with Torstar for Canadian programmatic scale ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/04/3093529/0/en/Torstar-and-MediaTonik-pair-up-for-programmatic-scale-that-s-truly-Canadian.html)). Canada only, Quebec headquartered, SME sized.

### They are genuinely building AI, but not the AI you build

MediaTonik was selected as one of 14 Quebec companies in **IVADO's DémultiplIA 2026 cohort**, a programme run by the Institut de valorisation des données (Université de Montréal, with Polytechnique, HEC, Laval and McGill) and funded by Quebec's economy ministry. It gives them scientific and entrepreneurial mentorship, custom collaborative research projects, HEC MBA student engagements and access to research talent. Entry requires becoming an IVADO member and committing a minimum of **$30,000 per year** to collaborative research ([Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2026/02/02/mediatonik-demultiplia-2026-program/), [IVADO](https://ivado.ca/en/2026/01/28/demultiplia-2026-14-quebec-companies-powered-by-ai/)).

The direction is stated plainly by the president:

> "Our participation in the DémultiplIA program is part of a clear vision to make MediaTonik a global leader in intelligent advertising monetization. This program represents a unique opportunity to accelerate our transition to a **predictive advertising network**, combining artificial intelligence, cutting-edge academic research and field expertise for the benefit of the publishers and advertisers we support every day."
> Nicolas Faucher, quoted in [Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2026/02/02/mediatonik-demultiplia-2026-program/)

**Predictive advertising monetisation is adtech machine learning: yield optimisation, bid prediction, audience segmentation.** It is not generative media. There is no public sign of MediaTonik generating images, video or copy.

### The people

- **Nicolas Faucher**: cofounder and president. The quote above is his. Twitter/X handle [@faumedia](https://twitter.com/faumedia) ([Media in Canada tag page](https://mediaincanada.com/tag/nicolas-faucher/)).
- **Christopher (Chris) Rovny**: Chief Strategy Officer. He was CEO and president of Obox and became CSO of MediaTonik after the acquisition ([ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Christopher-Rovny/11020288924), [Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2023/04/11/mediatonik-acquires-oboxs-ad-network/)).
- **Patrick Forget**: described in the DémultiplIA article's photo caption as **tech lead** at MediaTonik, not Head of Engineering & Technology. Possibly a recent promotion, possibly a caption simplification. **Unverified.** He is the only engineering person of the three and therefore the only one whose problems overlap yours at all.
- Also worth knowing: **Guillaume Bédard** is the other cofounder and appears in the same photo ([Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2026/02/02/mediatonik-demultiplia-2026-program/)).

### Verdict

A real AI-building company with real money committed to R&D, but building the wrong kind of AI for you. Adtech ML, not generative media. Talk to Patrick Forget if he is standing there, skip the executives.

## Ranking

### Tier 1: go find them

**1. Brigitte Monneau (Bridge Média / Entertain-AI)**

She runs the organisation that convenes every Quebec company doing what you want to do, she is actively building the E-AI 2027 programme right now, and her whole professional identity is connecting people across the industry. She has no technical filter, which means she will evaluate you on whether you are interesting and useful to her community, and three working generative media pipelines is interesting. The Ozaki introduction is worth using even though I could not confirm it, because a warm opener from anyone costs nothing.

The realistic outcome here is not a job. It is a speaker or ALCHIMIA connection at E-AI 2027 and introductions to Moment Factory, Reimagine AI and the rest.

> **Opener:** "You are putting E-AI 2027 together now. I build generative media pipelines, systems that take a data source and produce finished video and images automatically, and one of them cuts a two-hour live gig recording into captioned reels with no human in the loop. Who in the E-AI community is actually building tooling rather than using it, and is there a place on the 2027 programme for someone showing a working pipeline rather than a slide deck?"

That question does two things: it hands her the thing she needs (programme content) and it asks her the question only she can answer (who builds versus who buys).

**2. Charles Trahan (Québecor)**

Ranked second only because the evidence is thin, not because the fit is worse. "Directeur Innovation & IA" at the largest francophone media group in Canada is the most relevant job title on this list. But Groupe TVA is in the middle of heavy layoffs, and you have no idea whether his mandate is content production, internal productivity, or corporate strategy. Resolve that in the first two minutes, then decide whether to keep talking.

> **Opener:** "Is your mandate on the production side, actually making content faster, or on the business side? I ask because I build pipelines that take a data source and output finished video and images, and I cannot tell from the outside whether Québecor is building that kind of thing internally or buying it."

### Tier 2: worth the time if they are in front of you

**3. Christophe Cluzel (Radio-Canada)**

Not a hiring path, a network path. He spoke at E-AI 2025, so he corroborates and extends the Monneau lead independently. Also the person who can point you at Radio-Canada's actual AI-in-content people.

> **Opener:** "You are a year into DémultiplIA. What did the IVADO connection actually change in your engineering practice, and did the academic research translate into shipped product or stay in the lab?"

**5. Jordan Monaghan (Angle Media Group)**

Only because of the July 2026 AI dubbing launch. He is the decision maker at a shop that just put generative media into a paid client deliverable, which makes him one of very few people at this conference with a real commercial opinion on it. Ten employees in Toronto means no job, but he will tell you what actually broke.
